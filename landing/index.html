FAQs

Everything You Need to Know

What operational areas do you serve in Laguna?
We deliver professional services exclusively to private residences, secured subdivisions, luxury condominiums, and hotel rooms within Cabuyao, Sta. Rosa, Calamba, and Biñan.
How does a home-service massage work? What do I need to prepare?
You do not need to prepare anything. Our licensed therapists arrive with professional massage beds, sanitized linens, natural warming oils, and therapeutic ambient music. You only need to provide a comfortable room with sufficient space to set up the massage bed.
Is there a minimum booking requirement for a home session?
Yes, to maintain operational viability, ensure fair compensation for our therapists, and offset travel expenses, we implement a strict minimum booking of 2 hours per therapist per household.
What are your payment guidelines?
We keep payment simple. You can pay cash directly to your therapist, send a GCash mobile-wallet transfer, or pay by direct bank transfer. We confirm the exact amount with you over Messenger before your session.
How do you handle estate security clearances and gated subdivisions?
When submitting your booking request, please provide details on gate access rules, condominium protocols, or visitor passes. Our dispatch team coordinates with you to pre-clear our therapists with subdivision guardhouses, ensuring a smooth arrival.
